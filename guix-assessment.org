#+title: Guix assessment

This is a personal assessment based on several years of using and occasionally contributing to GNU Guix. The scores are subjective and are intended as notes about the project's technical design, development process and governance rather than as recommendations for or against using Guix.

* Rating legend

- Score = How well Guix handles this feature or property.
- Impact = How strongly this feature or property affects Guix as a distribution and development project overall, considering users and contributors.
- Scope = The group or level most directly affected.

| Score | Meaning                                                            |
|-------+--------------------------------------------------------------------|
|     5 | Excellent - a clear strength; unusually well designed or executed. |
|     4 | Good - works well, with noticeable but limited shortcomings.       |
|     3 | Mixed - meaningful strengths and weaknesses; broadly adequate.     |
|     2 | Poor - substantial shortcomings or recurring practical problems.   |
|     1 | Very poor - fundamentally deficient, dysfunctional or harmful.     |

| Impact | Meaning                                                            |
|--------+--------------------------------------------------------------------|
|      5 | Very high - foundational or project-wide consequences.             |
|      4 | High - substantial and recurring consequences.                     |
|      3 | Moderate - noticeable consequences in ordinary use or development. |
|      2 | Limited - affects narrower workflows or subsets of people.         |
|      1 | Low - mostly cosmetic, occasional or peripheral.                   |
